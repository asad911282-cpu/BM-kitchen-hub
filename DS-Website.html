Biryani Master Supply Manager

Daily Kitchen Stock — Website. The same live record as the apps — an order placed here appears in the Kitchen app, and a Kitchen accept here appears in the Restaurant app. Login first, then your workspace opens on this one page.

Supply Manager — Admin Log In

Admin only: admin@biryanimaster.com

First login — set your Admin password below (min 4 characters).

Log In — Restaurant

Log in with your ID + website PIN. Your branch stays locked after login.

First Time on This Website?

Your app PIN lives only on your phone, so this website cannot check it. Enter the ID + the mobile number you registered in the app — if they match the Admin’s record, set a 4-digit PIN for this website. You only do this once; afterwards log in above with your ID + website PIN.

Today’s Order —

Enter each dish in two columns: Rice / Tota Rice kg on the left, protein kg on the right. Each row = 1 Patila. Zarda is Rice only. Daal Chawal is Tota Rice + Daal, and its Tota patila and Daal patila are counted separately. Placing an order sends it to your Kitchen — then it is locked.

Kitchen Stock — Remaining (read-only)

You can see your Kitchen’s stock, but you cannot enter or change it.

My Orders

Placed orders cannot be edited or deleted. If the Kitchen rejects one, enter it again in Today’s Order. Only the Admin can change a placed entry.

Daily Report

WhatsApp share sends the report as a picture/PDF file with a short summary — the full report is never stuffed into a link, so nothing gets cut off.

Incoming Orders —

Orders from your paired Restaurant only. Accept or Reject each one — a rejected order needs a reason, and then the Restaurant can enter it again.

🔔 Pending order — alert tone is ON until you Accept or Reject.

My Stock —

Your own stock only — no dish entries here. Saved entries are locked: only the Admin can change them afterwards.

Previous Stock (پچھلا اسٹاک)

Set once at the start of the day. After saving it locks.

Stock In (وصول)

Chicken comes in shoppers — 1 shopper = 7 kg chicken. Other materials come in direct kg.

Out-Stock (بھیجا ہوا)

Anything the Kitchen sends out — e.g. Staff Food. Each saved row is locked.

Send to Other Branch (دوسری برانچ کو بھیجیں)

Send a dish or a single item to another branch. It deducts from your stock here — Daal Chawal deducts Tota Rice + Daal, never normal Rice. Your Staff Food Out-Stock above stays as it is.

Daily Report

WhatsApp share sends the report as a picture/PDF file with a short summary — the full report is never stuffed into a link, so nothing gets cut off.

Branch Pairs

One Restaurant + one Kitchen per pair. Orders route only inside a pair.

Add a New Pair

Users & Devices — Branch Assignments

Devices appear here after a user finishes first-time setup. Only you can change a device’s branch — it is recorded in the audit log.

Items & Recipes

The 7 orderable dishes are fixed. You can add extra recipes here for the record (they are not orderable).

Settings — Entries

ALL entries are listed below — dish orders and Kitchen stock entries, with branch and date. Tap any entry to edit, void or delete it. Every change needs a note and is written to the audit log. Only the Admin can change a placed entry.
