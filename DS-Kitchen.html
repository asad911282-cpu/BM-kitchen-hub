First-Time Setup — Kitchen

Set up once. Your ID is permanent and your branch is locked after setup — only the Supply Manager Admin can change it. If this branch already has a user, your ID goes to your Restaurant for approval first — you can log in once they approve it.

Log In — Kitchen

Log in with your ID + PIN.

Incoming Orders —

Orders from your paired Restaurant only. Accept or Reject each one — a rejected order needs a reason, and then the Restaurant can enter it again.

🔔 Pending order — alert tone is ON until you Accept or Reject.

My Stock —

Your own stock only — no dish entries here. Saved entries are locked: only the Admin can change them afterwards.

Previous Stock (پچھلا اسٹاک)

Set once at the start of the day. After saving it locks.

Stock In (وصول)

Chicken comes in shoppers — 1 shopper = 7 kg chicken. Other materials come in direct kg.

Self-Pickup Patilas (پتیلا)

Record patilas going out — your van rider or a customer picking up. Count is required; patila numbers and where they are going are optional. Only you can mark a patila returned when it comes back.

Returned Log

Out-Stock (بھیجا ہوا)

Anything the Kitchen sends out — e.g. Staff Food. Each saved row is locked.

Send to Other Branch (دوسری برانچ کو بھیجیں)

Send a dish or a single item to another branch. It deducts from your stock here — Daal Chawal deducts Tota Rice + Daal, never normal Rice. Your Staff Food Out-Stock above stays as it is.

Daily Report

WhatsApp share sends the report as one formatted PDF file with a short summary — the full report is never stuffed into a link, so nothing gets cut off.
